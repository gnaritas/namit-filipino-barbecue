From a food truck to a front door

Years of skewers. Now a place to call home.

Nanit began in 2022 as a food truck, popping up around Anne Arundel and Howard counties with skewers of charcoal-grilled meat alongside Filipino staples. The truck regularly sold out — demand kept outrunning what the grill could carry in a day.

In October 2026 the truck parked for good. On November 20, 2026, Namit opened its first brick-and-mortar restaurant at 9200 Old Annapolis Road, near Routes 29 and 108 — with help from a loan, grant and counseling through the Howard County Economic Development Authority.

On the grill

The dishes Namit is known for

Inasal

The charcoal-grilled chicken Bacolod is famous for — seasoned, skewered, flame-kissed.

Sisig

Sizzling chopped pork with its crisp-edged, squeeze-of-calamansi way of serving itself.

Lumpia

Crispy golden spring rolls — the party plate of every Filipino fiesta.

Pancit

Stir-fried noodles, long served for long lives.

Lechon

Pork roasted till the skin crackles — the celebration centerfold.

The kitchen

Maria Carmel & Paolo — chef and owner, both

Maria Carmel Tabligan and Paolo Peñalosa — who also goes by Pawie Pen — own Namit and cook it together. They built its following one skewer at a time from the truck, and the regulars who lined up for it are the reason there is now a front door to walk through.

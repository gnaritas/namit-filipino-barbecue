What "Filipino barbecue" means here

Filipino barbecue is its own tradition: meats seasoned bright with vinegar and calamansi, colored with achuete, grilled hard over coals. Inasal — the chicken Bacolod grill masters are known for — is Namit's signature. Sisig brings the sizzle: chopped pork with crisp edges, served hot enough to crack an egg on it. Lechon is the celebration: pork roasted till the skin crackles.

From the truck's grill to this one

For years, whatever Namit grilled sold out before the day was done — demand kept outrunning the truck. The storefront grill is the answer to that: owners and chefs Maria Carmel Tabligan and Paolo Peñalosa built the first kitchen so the skewers stop running out.
